### About

I've spent six years building data and reporting systems for a B2B SaaS company. My work sits between
the database and the people who make decisions: cleaning and reconciling business data, tracking
subscriptions and retention, making slow analytics fast, and automating the reports and alerts teams
use every day. I validate numbers before they ship and document systems so the next person can run them.

### What I work on

- **Data quality:** merging and reconciling records from several sources into one trusted client list
- **Subscription analytics:** cohort retention, MRR / NRR, churn and reactivation tracking
- **Performance:** rewriting slow queries and moving heavy workloads to columnar databases
- **Automation:** scheduled pipelines and chat alerts that explain *why* a metric moved
- **AI-assisted workflows:** LLM-based classification with rule layers and evaluation
